# Acme Corp Employee Handbook & Policies (2026)

## 1. Annual Leave & Holidays
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual vacation leave per calendar year. Leave accrues on the first day of each month at approximately 2.08 days per month. Unused vacation leave can be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar year, and must be utilized before March 31.

## 2. Remote Work & Home Office Stipend
Employees are eligible for hybrid or full-time remote work based on managerial approval. The company provides a one-time home office setup stipend of $1,000 for hardware, ergonomic furniture, and desk equipment. In addition, a monthly internet allowance of $60 is reimbursed upon submitting monthly billing receipts through the finance portal.

## 3. Working Hours & Core Collaboration Time
Standard working hours are 40 hours per week. While schedules are flexible, core collaboration hours across all engineering teams are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST) to accommodate team standups and cross-functional planning.

## 4. Health & Wellness Benefits
Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage begins on the first day of the month following the hire date. The company covers 90% of the employee premium and 75% of dependent premiums. Employees also receive a complimentary annual subscription to a mental health and meditation app.

## 5. Travel & Expense Reimbursement
All business-related travel must be booked through the corporate travel concierge at least 14 days in advance for domestic flights and 30 days in advance for international flights. The daily meal per diem cap is $75 for domestic travel and $110 for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable under standard corporate travel guidelines.

## 6. Intellectual Property & Code Contributions
All software, designs, inventions, and documentation created during employment and using company resources remain the exclusive property of Acme Corp. Contributing to open-source software using personal time and non-company hardware is permitted provided it does not compete with Acme Corp products or reveal confidential proprietary algorithms.
